## 3. Independently specified candidate language contract — not adopted

This section defines the minimum candidate interface for an eventual lock. It selects no numeric scientific threshold, final sample, seed or custodian.

1. **Identity and ingestion:** bind each record to experiment/protocol/parser/model/tokenizer/checkpoint hashes, seed, arm, fixed inference-budget point, item ID and condition. Join by keys, never file order. Require exactly the committed item/seed/arm coverage; reject duplicate/missing keys and nonfinite/invalid aggregate rows. Keep failed generations as zero-quality records, not dropped denominators. Instrument-invalid annotations are separately flagged, never converted into model errors or favorable exclusions.
2. **Answer contract:** common prompt requests the final short answer only. Preserve original raw tokens/text and engine termination trace. Candidate normalization is NFC, whitespace collapse and whole-answer exact comparison against privately reviewed aliases; preserve case, punctuation, units, digits and signs. No regex/favorable substring, model-dependent correction or outcome-mined aliases. Prelock EOS/tokenizer IDs, stop strings and whether the engine removes them, max-new-token budget and raw-token stop detection. Invalid/empty/truncated/error outputs fail even if a string looks correct. This strict candidate can reject semantically correct alternate forms; resolve task suitability/aliases on development before lock, never after final outcomes. It does not change E20's separately nominated normalization.
3. **Independent difficulty:** two annotators blinded to model outputs/loss/energy specify grounded evidence spans, rule identities and the minimal essential dependency DAG; record disagreements, adjudication and shorter solutions. Premises have depth zero and serial depth is one plus maximum essential-parent depth. Treat parallel arity, distinct required facts, context/question/answer length, topic/templates and distractor/nuisance cues separately. Balance or stratify these factors and demonstrate answer-changing essential-premise interventions and answer-preserving nuisance pairs on public development items. A matched trivial retrieval/shortcut control must not explain the supposed hard class. Hard cutoff, quotas and disagreement policy remain prospective decisions, not invented values.
4. **Energy/gauge:** pin the actual potential (`e21_mechanism.py:99–103`), z0=0, full head/base/readout/lift/features, dtype, normalizations, alpha, projection radius/restoring term, checkpoints and teacher-forced scored-context mask. Low energy is favorable; higher authored difficulty is harder. Compute per-item means on the **same** teacher-forced contexts, not arm-dependent free-running trajectories. Use average-rank Spearman with explicit ties; constant/too-small cases INVALID, not rho=0 or PASS. Keep correlations within a seed/checkpoint; independently learned energy scales are not automatically poolable. Pin projected residual `||z-project(z-alpha*grad_z E)||/alpha`, fixed K and development-locked numerical stationarity tolerance before final execution. Energy-drop and residual are diagnostics, not replacement M1 gates.
5. **Causal scoring:** preserve actual shifted valid-target counts and sum CE/count, exponentiating once for conditional PPL. Teacher-forced ground-truth prefixes are valid only for conditional NLL and must never enter free-running generation beyond generated tokens. Independent aliases/difficulty/metadata/lineage must not reach inference or training. Score actual generated answers separately from teacher-forced energy diagnostics; do not average their units.
6. **Control comparability:** same frozen backbone/readout/lift/context features, function-identical K=0, paired initialization/data/order and functional trainable counts. Require both direct recurrent and separately trained one-step controls; frozen/random energy are diagnostics, not substitutes. The scalar mechanism has nested derivatives and a conservative update field; the direct field need not be conservative. Equal counts do not imply equal expressivity or FLOPs. Preserve equal-exposure diagnostics **and** development-measured equal-cost training/inference points as distinct views; include base encoding, candidate differentiation, telemetry, warmup, repeats, tuning and full generation. Extra final-energy tracing head calls must be charged or consistently isolated from latency. Fixed LoRA/replay are strong practical controls; omission needs an approved restricted claim, not a silent replacement by a weak baseline.
7. **Custody and lineage:** require privately checked E21 source/page/QID/revision/import/near-duplicate lineage separation from E19/E20/development, including E20's private corpus, without exposing final identifiers/aliases to learners. Independently review model/code/corpus rights and human annotation provenance. Temporal freshness does not establish unseen facts or absent pretraining copies. Accepted independent principal/off-host custodian, actual non-sensitive denied-read probes for each training/tool principal, privilege/key/mount review and one fixed read-only all-arm release remain compulsory. No final examples/keys in this channel.

Gauge remains a substantive limitation: `E+c(h)` preserves candidate gradients/predictions but can reverse raw energy/difficulty ranks. Pinning an absolute convention makes the statistic reproducible, **not calibrated evidence of reasoning**. A diagnostic energy drop is offset-invariant on identical contexts; promoting it or replacing M1 requires an explicit pre-outcome owner-approved amendment.
