Abstract

We study a memory-first alternative to in-context learning for a pinned 360M-parameter language model, run entirely on CPU. Experience is encoded into content keys (last-token hidden states), stored in a growing key-value memory, and retrieved at inference time by kernel regression with adaptive bandwidth β = β₁·n^α + β₀. The retrieval feature feeds an energy head; its learned descent step is inspired by the predictive-coding loop of VCNet (arXiv 2508.02995) — an analogy we tested, not a proven identity for this learned MLP energy (see Discussion). Measured on a 16-query, 4-class source-work classification with anonymous labels: the mosaic retrieval arm improves monotonically with shots (0 → 37.5% at 8 shots, crossing chance) while a frozen no-memory baseline stays flat at 12.5%; adding the adaptive-β retrieval feature to the energy head lifts accuracy from 56.25% to 68.75% at 8 shots, beating the direct head (56.25%) at matched head evaluations. An earlier 75.0% figure is retracted: the energy loss was detached from the energy head, so that number came from the output classifier alone (correction, receipt8). Two negative results are reported in full: the naive VCNet centroid-classifier transplant fails (12.5% vs 37.5%), and the learnable β schedule did not train in the receipt6 implementation; the cause was measured (raw squared distances between 960-dim keys saturate the kernel softmax) and the repaired schedule trains (receipt8). A same-size competitor baseline is published: Qwen3-0.6B (596M params) reaches 37.5% max on the same fixture at 137.7–591.7 prompt tokens per query, while the repaired energy arm reaches 68.75% at ~0 prompt tokens — an exploratory development-fixture result, not yet held-out same-size superiority. A preregistered balanced held-out split (8 queries, 2 per class, disjoint from all dev sets) was then run: the energy arm reaches 75% at 8 shots against Qwen3-0.6B at 25%, but the acceptance gate fails on per-class coverage (0 of 2 on pg2701) and the no-memory direct arm ties at 75% — held-out superiority is NOT established (receipt11, published as a negative result). Methods

Model: pinned SmolLM2-360M (weights SHA-256 7aaff666…), float32, CPU-only, device-placement attested in every receipt, 0 GPU allowance. Fixture: 64 passages from 4 source works, anonymous labels (semantic-prior stripping), 16 held-out queries, shots n = 1/2/4/8, chance = 25%. Content key = last-token hidden of a 48-token excerpt (960-dim). Arms: K0 frozen baseline; mosaic label-vote with kernel weights softmax(−β·d²); energy head (E21 mechanism: energy E(z), K = 4 descent steps, radius 2.0); direct head; VCNet predictive-coding, gated, and recurrent variants; L1 = energy/direct heads with the adaptive-β retrieval feature appended to the head input. Training: 60 Adam steps, lr 0.02, 8 passages per step, at n = 4 (n = 1 has zero gradient for gain — measured).

Correction. Independent audit found that in the receipt6 implementation every energy update was detached (test_l1_adaptive_beta.py:99–103), so the classification loss never reached the energy-head parameters: all four energy-head gradients were None and every parameter change was exactly zero, while the direct head trained. Receipt6's 75.0% is therefore kept as a historical classifier-only result and is not evidence of trained-energy superiority. The repair (test_l1_adaptive_beta2.py) unrolls the energy descent differentiably (create_graph=True) and makes the norm clamp safe at z = 0, which removed the NaN gradients on Fc/qv. The measured cause of the inert β schedule was also found: raw squared distances between 960-dim keys are ~1e3, so softmax(−β·d²) saturates to one-hot and the gradient w.r.t. β₁, α, β₀ is exactly −0.0; normalising d² by its mean makes the schedule trainable. Repaired result: energy-head gradients finite and nonzero at step 0 (Fc 0.6772, qv 0.0009, head0 0.1119, head1 0.0018, head2 0.0095, head3 0.0059 — exact values from the receipt8 JSON), all parameter deltas > 0, β schedule trains (β₁ 0.5847, α 0.5948, β₀ 0.0761 → β = 0.66/0.96/1.41/2.09 at n = 1/2/4/8). Energy 56.25/68.75/62.5/68.75% vs direct 43.75/62.5/50/56.25%. Energy ≥ direct at matched head evaluations (2176 each), and energy pays 2176 extra second-order gradient calls — equal Adam steps is not equal compute, so the comparison is reported with the true counts. Remaining open defect: the shot curve is still non-monotone.

Discussion

The energy descent step has the same algebraic shape as the predictive-coding update (a displacement driven by a prediction residual), and the VCNet paper describes that loop as a geodesic toward a kernel-weighted prediction. We use that as inspiration and analogy: for the learned MLP energy E(z) used here, no implementation-specific proof has been produced that the descent is the VCNet geodesic or moves z toward the kernel-weighted centroid, and we do not claim one. The centroid classifier after refinement is the measured bottleneck of the naive transplant. The β schedule is now trainable once d² is normalised (cause measured above), and it moves in both arms. The repaired energy arm beats the direct arm at matched head evaluations; the curve peaks at n = 2 and n = 8 (68.75%) and is still non-monotone. The n = 1 value (56.25%) sits below n = 2: at one shot, shots_for() selects a fixed pool shot (the first candidate of class a), so w = 1 is trivial single-entry weighting — retrieval at n = 1 is not the nearest key, and no causal explanation of the curve shape has been demonstrated. Recorded as an open defect.

Limitations

16 queries, 4 classes, one 360M model, CPU. Accuracy at 8 shots is 68.75% in the repaired arm — far from production quality, and the margin over the direct head is 12.5 points on 16 queries, i.e. two queries. The "highest quality" axis is gated on owner-side prerequisites not yet completed: 704-QA human curation, named custody, E20 rights/model-bytes/fit. No leap claim is made; the falsifiable claim (matched-compute vs fine-tuning, L3) has not been run.

Next (pre-registered)

L2: growing memory store + distillation loop, acceptance = tokens-per-task falls while accuracy holds. L3: matched-compute comparison vs full fine-tuning on the pinned 360M, acceptance = quality ≥ fine-tune at ≤ 1/10 compute, retention ≥ gate. Each lands here as a receipt when verified.
