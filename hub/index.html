NEW AI / AN OPEN RESEARCH PROGRAM

Can a model learn without erasing?

We are testing whether iterative inference and controlled adaptation can add capability while retaining what came before. The architecture is a hypothesis, not a running self-evolving language model. So far, the measured timing test failed.

02 / CLAIM LEDGER

The model does not grade itself.

Each architectural claim needs a matched counterfactual and a test the training system cannot rewrite. Energy going down is only a mechanism trace; held-out task quality and measured cost decide whether the mechanism is useful.

6 claims shown
M1 / INFERENCE

Does energy descent help?

Compare a trained scalar-energy latent update to an equally sized direct recurrent update and a frozen bypass, on the same language tasks and measured compute.

Falsifier: energy falls without held-out quality gain, or the direct update matches it. Original-source review.

E21 · unrun
M2 / TIMING

Does the trigger earn its place?

The E19 classifier compared feedback, scheduled and rate-matched random adapter growth on the same held-out news-text task.

Feedback equalled schedule on every seed; the preregistered timing gate failed. E20 proposes replay, capacity and routing controls on an actual LM. Raw E19 data.

E19 · failed
M3 / RETENTION

What survives adaptation?

Measure old- and general-domain language loss and grounded-answer quality before and after each change, not just whether core tensors stayed frozen.

Falsifier: new ability arrives only by degrading old tasks or selecting the best expert with an unavailable oracle. E20 design.

E20 · draft
M4 / EFFICIENCY

Is the gain worth its cost?

Account for backbone, detector, routing, replay, optimizer and full autoregressive generation, including p95 latency and actual peak memory.

Equal token counts are not equal FLOPs. A cost advantage cannot be claimed without measured, comparable end-to-end work. Measurement plan.

E20 · draft
M5 / SAFETY

Can a candidate replace the active model?

Two-model promotion would keep an active checkpoint separate from a candidate while an independent evaluator checks retention and failure cases.

Rollback must restore a verified prior checkpoint. This is a future control design, not an implemented deployment or measured safety guarantee. Master plan.

Not built
M6 / SUBSTRATE

Can it grow without a memory wall?

Ternary arithmetic, expert paging and pruning are separate systems hypotheses that require their own quality, throughput, latency and integrity tests.

No production ternary LM kernel, autonomous cold-storage graft or unbounded-growth result exists here. Experiment inventory.

Not built
03 / NEXT EXPERIMENTS

Two tests. Two different questions.

The next language benchmark and the energy mechanism ablation must not be blended into one optimistic score. Both require frozen protocols and an inaccessible final test before any confirmatory run.

E20 / proposed benchmark · not sealed

When should capacity change?

A pretrained small language model on a fresh, group-disjoint prose stream, tested against frozen, fixed LoRA, replay, schedule, random-timed and feedback-timed controls.

  • Readouts conditional suffix loss, grounded QA, retention, false events
  • Cost end-to-end wall/device time, memory and generation latency
  • Gate must beat matched controls, not only the frozen model

Read proposed evaluator design ↗

E21 / separate proposal · not run

Does the energy update do more?

On a frozen pretrained LM, train a scalar latent potential and compare actual candidate-gradient refinement to a parameter-matched direct vector update.

  • Readouts held-out language loss, free-running generation and old-task retention
  • Control K=0 bypass, direct update, one-step head, random-energy diagnostic
  • Gate match measured compute before crediting the gradient mechanism

Read original-source analysis ↗

Before any outcome run.

Owner choice: approve the E20 scope, a resource ceiling and human-verified QA preparation—or ask for a different full-method replication.

Evaluator lock: data rights and grouping, inaccessible final split, model/runner hashes, acceptance rules and development-only feasibility. No result or future design on this page is a sealed E20 or E21 outcome.

04 / SOURCE INTAKE · INCOMPLETE

Geometry can test a claim. It cannot prove the whole model.

The complete returned video caption track and both supplied geometry papers have been reviewed. Their ideas separate into a controlled reasoning instrument, external symbolic assistance, and a still-unspecified internal training hypothesis. The four additional philosophy-paper versions are under review; none is a New AI model result.

E22 / possible future instrument · not approved

Make the reasoning test falsifiable.

A clean-room fact-and-rule generator could vary proof depth, branching and distractors. Pair answer-changing premises with answer-preserving nuisances, and require both answers and derivations to pass an independent oracle.

Compare original prompts, typed facts, relevant and irrelevant deductions, a deliberately wrong fact, and an exact tool with the same declared model and measured budgets. A useful external assistant is not proof of a new internal learning mechanism.

BOUNDARY / NOT A SOPHONTIC REPLICATION

Do not confuse three meanings of geometry.

GeomVerse is a benchmark; symbolic diagram-to-theorem guidance is an inference-time pipeline; the interview discusses internal representations without a reproducible training recipe. Any structured-representation training test here would be our own separately specified method, with ordinary supervised and contrastive controls.
